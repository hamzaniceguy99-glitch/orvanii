The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 10 weeks, each aimed at getting one real room finished.

Program 01 · From stuck to done

One room, finished

The complete program. We start with measurements and a simple plan, fix the layout before anything is bought, then work through furniture, lighting and textiles in an order that avoids expensive mistakes. You do the buying and the installing; we make sure it's the right thing at the right time.

  • 8 weeks
  • Any room
  • 3 hours a week
  • Tape measure and phone

Program 02 · Choose with confidence

Color & light

Paint looks different in every room, and most homes are lit by a single overhead light that flatters nothing. We test colors properly on your walls, build a three-layer lighting plan, and get the bulb temperature consistent so everything stops looking slightly off.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any room
  • 2 hours a week
  • Paint samples

Start this program

Program 03 · The finishing ten percent

Styling & decor

For rooms with all the furniture and none of the atmosphere. We work on scale and grouping, where art actually belongs on a wall, and how to layer textiles so a space feels lived in rather than staged. Mostly using things you already own.

  • 6 weeks
  • Furnished rooms
  • 2 hours a week
  • What you own

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · Edit

Removing what's fighting, grouping what works.

Weeks 3–4 · Walls

Art size, height and arrangement that isn't random.

Weeks 5–6 · Layers

Textiles, plants and the last details.

Weeks 1–2 · Ranking

What changes the room most per dollar spent.

Weeks 3–4 · Sourcing

Secondhand, floor models, and what to buy new.

Weeks 5–6 · Ordering

Measuring twice, lead times, and return policies.

Program 04 · Spend where it shows

Budget & sourcing

Budget decides more than taste does. We rank your room's purchases by impact, work out what's worth buying well and what genuinely isn't, and check measurements before anything is ordered — the mistake that costs people the most. We recommend no retailers and take no commissions.

  • 6 weeks
  • Any budget
  • 2 hours a week
  • A budget figure
