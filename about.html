How we work

Decisions, in the right order

Most stuck rooms have the same history: a few purchases made in the wrong sequence, a rug that's too small, and a growing reluctance to spend anything else until it's certain.

Coaching removes the uncertainty one decision at a time. Layout before furniture, size before style, and a second pair of eyes on anything before it's ordered.

We also keep the scope honest. Decorating is coaching; structural changes, wiring and permits belong with licensed professionals, and we'll say so.
